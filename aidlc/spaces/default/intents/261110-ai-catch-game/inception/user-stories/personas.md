# Personas — AI CATCH

Both personas come from the target audiences in the Intent Statement: hackathon judges and participants, and general web users (Intent Capture Q2) [Q1]. Names are placeholders.

## P1 — Jordan, the hackathon judge

- **Role**: Judge (or fellow participant) evaluating Kiroween 2026 entries in the Resurrection category
- **Goals**:
  - Understand the concept and see the whole game working within a few minutes
  - Judge how well the retro handheld experience has been resurrected (Design, Innovation/Idea)
  - See a complete, polished product rather than a technical prototype
- **Pain points**:
  - Has many entries to review and little time for each
  - Entries that need set-up, sign-in or explanation before they can be tried
  - Rough edges (broken screens, missing sound) that make a prototype feel unfinished
- **Context**: Usually on a laptop with a keyboard, opening the submitted URL in a desktop browser; may also try it on a phone
- **Tech comfort**: High
- **Frequency**: Once or twice during judging, a few minutes each time

## P2 — Sam, the casual web player

- **Role**: General web user who finds the game through a shared link
- **Goals**:
  - Start playing instantly, with no install or account
  - Enjoy short sessions and try to beat their own best score
  - Play on whatever device is at hand, phone or PC
- **Pain points**:
  - Games that load slowly or are heavy on mobile data
  - Controls that are awkward on a touch screen
  - Losing their best score or being interrupted by layout problems when the phone rotates
- **Context**: Often on a smartphone in spare moments, held in either orientation; sometimes on a PC
- **Tech comfort**: Medium
- **Frequency**: Several short sessions, repeated over days

## Relationship and Priority

- **P1 (judge)** is the primary persona for this version: the entry is built for the Kiroween 2026 hackathon (Intent Capture Q1), and the judges decide its outcome. Stories that make the first few minutes complete and polished matter most to Jordan.
- **P2 (casual player)** is the secondary persona: the game is meant to stay playable for general users after judging (Intent Capture Q2). Smartphone play, the high score and rotation matter most to Sam.
- Most Must Have stories serve both personas; the Should Have stories mainly serve Sam's repeated play.
