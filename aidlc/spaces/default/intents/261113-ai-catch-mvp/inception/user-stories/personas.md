# Personas: AI CATCH (four-stage MVP)

Derived from the target customer in `aidlc/spaces/default/intents/261113-ai-catch-mvp/ideation/intent-capture/intent-statement.md`, the hackathon context in the feasibility assessment, and story-plan answer Q1 in `user-stories-questions.md`.

## P1: Casual Player (primary)

- **Who**: Anyone who opens the public URL on a desktop browser or a smartphone, including people who know nothing about computing history.
- **Goals**: Understand the game within seconds, play it without reading instructions, feel progress, and want to try again.
- **Pain points**: Games that need tutorials or explanations; controls that feel unfair on touch screens; losing because of something they could not see.
- **Context**: Short sessions, often on a phone in portrait or landscape; may have sound off or blocked at first.
- **What success looks like**: Reaches at least Stage 2 on a first try, understands that each stage restores part of the AI, and restarts to beat their high score.

## P2: Hackathon Judge (secondary)

- **Who**: A Kiroween 2026 judge assessing the submission against impact, technical implementation, design, idea, and presentation.
- **Goals**: Play the whole game from title to victory ending in a few minutes, and see the story (humans restoring AI through computing history) come across through play.
- **Pain points**: Prototypes that feel unfinished; a story that is only told in text, not shown; a game that cannot be finished in a short review.
- **Context**: Desktop browser, keyboard, a limited time per entry; may also open it on a phone.
- **What success looks like**: Completes or nearly completes all four stages, sees "AI RESTORED — 100%" and "HUMANS SAVED AI.", and remembers the game as a cohesive product.

## Relationships and Priority

- **P1 is the primary persona**: every story must work for a first-time player with no instructions.
- **P2 is secondary**: P2's needs mostly reinforce P1's (clarity, polish, a finishable game). Where they pull apart, for example difficulty, Stage 1 stays gentle for P1, and later stages stay short enough for P2 to finish.
