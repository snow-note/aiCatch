# Intent Statement: AI CATCH

## Problem Statement

- The evolution of computing (FORTRAN, COBOL, BASIC) and modern AI (LLM, GPU, ROBOT) is hard to make understandable and memorable. AI CATCH addresses this with a simple, replayable browser game. [Q1]
- The game's story is that humans restore a corrupted AI. [Q1]

## Target Customer

- External players on desktop and mobile browsers, including people unfamiliar with computing history. [Q2]
- They want a game that is understandable immediately, without instructions. [Q2]

## Success Metrics

- A player can start the game, play all four stages, see the ending, and restart. [Q3]
- The game works on desktop (keyboard) and mobile (touch). [Q3]
- The game runs without any external AI service. [Q3]

## Initiative Trigger

- The initiative is an entry for the Kiroween 2026 hackathon, Resurrection category. [Q4]
- It revives the simple experience of classic handheld electronic games in a 2045 AI-themed world. [Q4]
- The initial concept is recorded in `original_spec.md`. [Q4]

## Initial Scope Signal

- Workflow-selected scope: `mvp`. [scope]
- First-version boundary: no rankings or leaderboards, no user accounts, no online multiplayer, no backend services or databases, no external AI APIs, and no features that do not support the core game experience. [Q8]
- Local high-score tracking is included in the first version. [Q8]
- The game is published through GitHub and Cloudflare. [Q8]
