# AI CATCH — Product Definition

## 1. Product Overview

**AI CATCH** is an original retro handheld-style catching game inspired by generations of computing history and the evolution of artificial intelligence.

The game combines simple, accessible gameplay with a narrative about humanity restoring a corrupted AI system by recovering the legacy of computing.

**Category:** Resurrection  
**Tagline:** SAVE THE AI. SAVE THE FUTURE.  
**Core Message:** Humans created AI. Now it's our turn to save it.

## 2. Product Vision and Core Message

AI CATCH stands on generations of computing history, programming languages, hardware innovations, and human ingenuity.

FORTRAN, COBOL, and BASIC represent important generations of computing technology that helped build the foundations of today's digital world.

Modern AI systems, including LLMs, GPUs, and robotics, have become powerful and influential. However, even the most advanced AI can be vulnerable to corruption and destruction.

When AI collapses, humanity must take responsibility for restoring it.

AI CATCH expresses the following message:

- Today's AI is built upon generations of computing innovation.
- Technological progress is connected across generations.
- Even advanced AI can be vulnerable.
- Humanity has the responsibility and ability to save AI.
- The future of AI depends on human ingenuity and the legacy of computing.

The game must communicate this message through its story, stage progression, gameplay, visual design, and ending.

## 3. Story

### Setting: 2045

The year is 2045.

Artificial intelligence has become an essential part of everyday life. Advanced LLMs, GPUs, and intelligent robots support civilization.

The AI system suddenly suffers a catastrophic VIRUS attack. Its intelligence becomes corrupted, and the AI system collapses.

The future that humanity built is now in danger.

HUMAN must recover the legacy of computing and restore the AI system.

By collecting historical computing technologies and modern AI components, HUMAN reconstructs the foundations of AI and gradually restores its intelligence.

The journey progresses through four stages, representing generations of computing history and the evolution of AI:

1. FORTRAN — Early scientific and mainframe computing.
2. COBOL — Business computing and information systems.
3. BASIC — Personal computing and accessible programming.
4. LLM — Modern artificial intelligence.

Each stage represents a part of the technological legacy needed to restore the corrupted AI.

The final objective is to restore AI to 100% and save the future.

### Narrative Principles

- Computing history is presented as a connected progression of technological innovation.
- FORTRAN, COBOL, and BASIC represent important historical generations, not direct or exclusive origins of modern AI.
- Modern AI is portrayed as the result of accumulated computing technology, human ingenuity, and continuous innovation.
- The VIRUS represents the vulnerability of even highly advanced technology.
- HUMAN represents humanity's responsibility, creativity, and ability to recover what has been lost.
- The story must remain understandable and engaging even for players unfamiliar with computing history.

## 4. Core Gameplay

AI CATCH is a simple, accessible, and replayable catching game.

The player controls HUMAN and moves horizontally to catch falling objects.

The player must collect the required objects for each stage while avoiding VIRUS enemies.

Successful collection increases the score and contributes to stage progression.

Each completed stage restores 25% of the corrupted AI system.

The game becomes progressively more challenging as the player advances through the stages.

The game must be understandable immediately and enjoyable without requiring instructions or prior knowledge of computing history.

The gameplay should reinforce the narrative of recovering computing history and restoring AI without introducing unnecessary complexity.

## 5. Stage Progression

The game consists of four stages.

### Stage 1 — FORTRAN

- **Theme:** Early scientific computing and mainframe computing.
- **Collectible:** Mainframe computers.
- **Objective:** Collect the required number of mainframe computers.
- **Narrative Role:** Recover the earliest computing foundation represented in the game.
- **Completion Reward:** Restore AI by 25%.
- **AI Restoration:** 0% → 25%.

### Stage 2 — COBOL

- **Theme:** Business computing and information systems.
- **Collectible:** Magnetic tapes.
- **Objective:** Collect the required number of magnetic tapes.
- **Narrative Role:** Recover the next generation of computing infrastructure and information systems.
- **Completion Reward:** Restore AI by an additional 25%.
- **AI Restoration:** 25% → 50%.

### Stage 3 — BASIC

- **Theme:** Personal computing and accessible programming.
- **Collectible:** Floppy disks.
- **Objective:** Collect the required number of floppy disks.
- **Narrative Role:** Recover the generation that made computing more accessible to people.
- **Completion Reward:** Restore AI by an additional 25%.
- **AI Restoration:** 50% → 75%.

### Stage 4 — LLM

- **Theme:** Modern artificial intelligence.
- **Collectibles:**
  - LLM
  - GPU
  - ROBOT
- **Objective:** Collect the required modern AI components.
- **Narrative Role:** Reconstruct the modern AI system using the recovered computing legacy and contemporary AI technologies.
- **Completion Reward:** Restore AI by the final 25%.
- **AI Restoration:** 75% → 100%.

### Stage Progression Rules

- All stages contain VIRUS enemies.
- Stage progression must increase gameplay difficulty through factors such as falling speed, virus frequency, and simultaneous falling objects.
- Stage objectives must remain clear and visually understandable.
- Stage transitions must communicate the recovered computing generation and the resulting AI restoration progress.
- Each completed stage restores exactly 25% of AI.
- Completing Stage 4 restores AI to 100% and triggers the victory ending.

Exact gameplay balancing parameters are defined during implementation and testing.

## 6. Game Rules

- HUMAN is the player-controlled character.
- HUMAN moves horizontally to catch falling objects.
- Collecting stage-specific objects increases the score and advances stage objectives.
- Stage 4 collectibles represent modern AI components and contribute to final AI restoration.
- VIRUS appears in every stage.
- Contact with VIRUS reduces the player's remaining lives by one.
- The game ends when the player's lives reach zero.
- Completing a stage increases AI restoration progress by 25%.
- Completing Stage 4 restores AI to 100% and triggers the victory ending.

## 7. Victory and Ending

The final victory represents humanity successfully restoring the corrupted AI system.

When Stage 4 is completed:

- AI restoration reaches 100%.
- The AI system returns to its restored state.
- A final victory animation and visual feedback are displayed.
- The game presents the following messages:

**AI RESTORED — 100%**

**HUMANS SAVED AI.**

**SAVE THE AI. SAVE THE FUTURE.**

The ending should reinforce the relationship between humanity, computing history, and the future of AI.

The final emotional message is that humanity created AI, and humanity has the ability and responsibility to save it.

## 8. Visual Identity

AI CATCH uses an original retro handheld electronic game aesthetic.

### Visual Characteristics

- Monochrome gray LCD display.
- Dark gray and black pixel graphics.
- Silver-gray handheld device casing.
- Minimalist pixel-art characters and objects.
- Digital segmented score display.
- Simple and readable game interface.
- Original retro electronic sound effects.
- Subtle LCD flicker, glitch, and corruption effects.

The game must prioritize gameplay readability over strict retro authenticity.

Collectible objects and VIRUS enemies must be visually distinguishable without relying on color alone.

### Narrative Visual Progression

Visual effects should support the story:

- Early stages represent historical computing technologies.
- The AI restoration percentage provides visible progression throughout the game.
- VIRUS effects communicate corruption and danger.
- Stage completion provides visual feedback that part of the AI has been restored.
- Stage 4 introduces modern AI components.
- The final victory communicates a transition from corrupted AI to restored AI.

All visual assets must be original.

## 9. Platform and Controls

The game is a browser-based HTML5 game.

Supported controls:

- **Keyboard:** Left and Right arrow keys.
- **Mobile:** Touch-based left and right movement controls.

The game must be playable on desktop and mobile browsers.

The gameplay must function without external AI services or API dependencies.

## 10. MVP Scope

The MVP must deliver a complete, playable, and polished game experience.

### Required Features

- Start screen.
- Four-stage progression.
- HUMAN movement and catching mechanics.
- Stage-specific collectible objects.
- VIRUS enemies in all stages.
- Score system.
- Lives and game-over conditions.
- Progressive difficulty.
- AI restoration progress from 0% to 100%.
- Stage completion feedback.
- Final victory and AI restoration ending.
- Restart functionality.
- Original retro visual effects and sound effects.
- Keyboard and mobile touch controls.
- Local high-score tracking.

The MVP must prioritize a cohesive and enjoyable gameplay experience over feature quantity.

### MVP Narrative Requirements

- The four stages must represent FORTRAN, COBOL, BASIC, and LLM.
- The collectible objects must correspond to their respective stages.
- Each completed stage must restore 25% of AI.
- Stage progression must communicate the recovery of computing history.
- The final stage must represent modern AI restoration.
- The ending must communicate that HUMAN has successfully saved AI.

The narrative must be delivered through lightweight in-game visual feedback and messaging rather than lengthy explanations or mandatory tutorials.

## 11. Scope Guardrails

- Keep gameplay simple, intuitive, and immediately accessible.
- Preserve the four-stage computing-history progression.
- Maintain the central theme of humanity restoring AI.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity and overengineering.
- Do not introduce online multiplayer, accounts, leaderboards, or backend services unless explicitly approved.
- Do not require external AI APIs for gameplay.
- Prioritize gameplay feel, visual clarity, sound, and replayability.
- Any additional feature must support the core game experience and MVP objectives.
- Do not expand MVP scope merely to demonstrate AI technology.
- The game must remain enjoyable and fully playable without AI-powered functionality.

## 12. Copyright and Originality

AI CATCH must be an original game.

- Do not reproduce existing copyrighted games, characters, sprites, music, sound effects, or distinctive interfaces.
- Do not use Nintendo Game & Watch branding, logos, or proprietary assets.
- Use original pixel-art characters, game objects, interface elements, animations, and sound effects.
- Third-party assets may only be used when their licenses permit the intended use.
- Record sources and licenses for external assets.
- Maintain an original visual identity inspired by the general aesthetic of classic monochrome handheld games.
- Do not use existing game assets or distinctive visual elements as direct references for reproduction.
- The product name and branding should be reviewed for potential trademark conflicts before public release.

4. The legacy of computing is recovered through FORTRAN, COBOL, and BASIC.
5. Modern AI components, including LLMs, GPUs, and robotics, are reconstructed.
6. HUMAN restores AI to 100%.
7. The future is saved through human ingenuity and generations of computing innovation.

### Core Promotional Message

**SAVE THE AI. SAVE THE FUTURE.**

Promotional materials must reinforce this narrative without contradicting the actual gameplay or implying unsupported game features.
